## my take

it is quite easy to get some happy feelings. get under people and drink


Of course, some things can make us feel good very quickly. A bottle of wine
might make an evening easier. Cocaine might give someone energy. But that is
not the same as happiness. Short-term pleasure can be easy. Long-term
happiness is harder.

I think happiness requires thinking beyond the next moment. It means building
a life that still feels worth living when things are uncertain, when politics
become frightening, when the economy feels stuck, or when you wake up and
simply do not feel like facing the day.

Even if you have “everything,” there will be days when it is hard.

i like to think about happiness as a deep feeling of gratefulness for what you
have achieved and what you gave the world and what the world gave back to you.

Maybe that is the real challenge of being happy in a European society: not
pretending that everything is fine, but learning how to stay hopeful,
connected, and responsible when it clearly is not.
