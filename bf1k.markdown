bf1k is a small hiking project from Tübingen.

We do regular day hikes to mountains, peaks and trails in the Black Forest
that reach over 1000 meters in elevation.

We want to spend more time outside, explore the region, and make it easier for
friends and people around Tübingen to join for a day in nature.

## how it works

A mountain counts as completed when we reach the top, open a beer, and take a
photo. The long-term goal is to do this on as many 1000m+ peaks across the
Black Forest as possible.

Depending on the route, we travel by train or by car. If I drive, I can
usually take up to four additional people.

If you’d like to join a future hike, just send me a [message](mailto:hannes.schoss@student.uni-tuebingen.de).

Please keep in mind that bf1k is informal and self-organized. Everyone joins
at their own responsibility. You should be comfortable spending several hours
outdoors, hiking at a steady pace, and walking on uneven terrain. Bring your
own food, water, proper shoes, and weather-appropriate clothing.
